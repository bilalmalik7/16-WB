Privacy Policy

Rise of the Numberdar · Last updated 23 October 2026

Summary

Rise of the Numberdar does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved games (autosave and three save slots) and settings (sound and voice preferences) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Voices

Character voices use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text of game dialogue is passed to that engine on your device. The game does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Internet access

The Android app works offline. It does not send any data over the internet.

Children

The game contains simulated gunfights and political themes and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
