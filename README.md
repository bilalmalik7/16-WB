# Rise of the Numberdar

A browser-based political strategy RPG. You play the Numberdar of Chak 47 Shumali, a village in Punjab, and try to rise from settling village disputes at your Dera to becoming President of Pakistan.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file with no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Everything else is inline.

To host it, enable **GitHub Pages** for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).

## How it works

- **Three stages:** The Village Dera → The MNA Campaign → The Presidency.
- **Four stats:** Izzat (respect), Khauf (fear), Paisa (wealth) and Ta'aluqat (connections), plus loyalty from the Jatt and Arain Biradaris.
- **Choices:** every scene offers three options. Some are locked until a stat is high enough.
- **Risks:** Khauf above 80 starts a rebellion. Paisa below zero or Izzat at zero ends the game.
- **Five endings:** President, Kingmaker, Lord of Chak 47, Bankrupt and The Empty Dera.
- **Cutscenes:** The Perfect Faisla, Results Night (MNA victory) and The Oath.
- **Controls:** click or press Space to advance dialogue. Press 1–3 to pick a choice. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
