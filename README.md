# Rise of the Numberdar

A browser-based political strategy RPG set in **Chak 16 WB, District Vehari**. You play the village Numberdar and try to rise from settling disputes at your Dera to becoming President of Pakistan, with Malik Tori as your rival all the way.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file with no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Everything else (art, animation, sound) is generated in the page.

To host it, enable **GitHub Pages** for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder).

## What's in it

- **Three stages and 22 story scenes:** The Village Dera → The MNA Campaign → The Presidency.
- **Storylines:**
  - canal-water faislas
  - a land grab at Murabba 14
  - the neza bazi horse mela
  - a Joiya–Kamboh blood feud
  - dacoits in the river belt
  - a face-off with Malik Tori on the canal bridge
  - Islamabad power politics
- **Two friends:** Hamza, a software engineer in Lahore who builds the Dera Helpline app, and Danish, an engineer in Frankfurt who rallies overseas Pakistanis behind your campaign.
- **Punjabi dialogue:** characters speak Roman Punjabi with optional English subtitles. Choices are in English.
- **Five biradaris:** Jatt, Arain, Rajput, Joiya and Kamboh each track their own loyalty. Enemies can become allies.
- **Stats:** Izzat (respect), Khauf (fear), Paisa (wealth) and Ta'aluqat (connections).
- **Risks:** Khauf above 80 starts a rebellion. Paisa below zero or Izzat at zero ends the game.
- **15 cutscenes:**
  - the opening flyover
  - land standoff, monsoon flood, The Perfect Faisla
  - neza bazi, canal-road ambush, horseback chase
  - Grand Jirga, Helpline launch, Germany
  - canal-bridge stare-down, water speech, polling day
  - MNA victory, National Assembly, Presidential oath
- **Sound:** all synthesized with the Web Audio API. It includes:
  - per-scene ambience: birds, crickets, rain, thunder, crowds, city
  - a tanpura and dhol score
  - character voice blips
  - effects: gunfire, hooves, helicopter, applause
- **Animation:** characters walk in and gesture when they speak. The camera follows whoever is speaking. Clouds, birds, dust, fireflies and lightning move in the background.
- **Seven endings:** President, Kingmaker, Lord of Chak 16 WB, The Deal, Bankrupt, The Empty Dera.
- **Controls:** click or press Space to advance dialogue. Press 1–4 to pick a choice. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
